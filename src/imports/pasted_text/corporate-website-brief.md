# Gilani Enterprises — Corporate Website Redesign

Design and build a complete, production-quality, responsive corporate website for **Gilani Enterprises**.

The website will eventually be exported/rebuilt in **WordPress**, so create the design and component architecture in a way that can be cleanly translated into reusable WordPress sections/components.

## 1. COMPANY POSITIONING

Gilani Enterprises is a U.S.-based technology and business organization with operations in **Oklahoma City, USA** and **Gujrat, Pakistan**.

The website's primary purpose is to position Gilani Enterprises as a trusted technology partner for small businesses in the United States.

The two primary service areas are:

### AI Consulting, Workflow Redesign & Automation

Help small businesses identify practical opportunities to use artificial intelligence in their existing operations.

Primary capabilities:

* AI-assisted business workflow redesign
* AI adoption consulting
* Business process analysis
* AI agents
* AI-powered business automation
* Workflow automation
* Integration of AI into existing business processes
* Identification of repetitive/manual processes that can be automated
* Practical AI solutions designed around business outcomes rather than technology for technology's sake

Do NOT invent specific AI platforms, certifications, partnerships, customer results, ROI percentages, case studies or client names.

### Technology Staff Augmentation & Offshore Development Teams

Help U.S. businesses expand their technology capabilities through experienced technology professionals and development teams based in Pakistan.

The positioning should emphasize:

* Technology staff augmentation
* Offshore development teams in Pakistan
* Flexible team expansion
* Access to skilled technology professionals
* Software developers
* QA/testing professionals
* DevOps professionals
* Business analysts
* Project managers
* AI engineers
* Data engineers
* Long-term development teams
* Project-based technical resources

Present Pakistan as a strategic technology delivery location rather than simply positioning the service as "cheap outsourcing."

Focus messaging on:

* access to talent
* flexibility
* scalability
* collaboration
* cost efficiency
* direct communication
* extending the client's existing team

Do NOT make unsupported claims about cost savings percentages, employee counts, certifications or number of developers.

## 2. SECONDARY COMPANY BACKGROUND

Gilani Enterprises has more than three decades of business and technology experience.

The previous website emphasized software development, consulting, technical staff augmentation, custom software development and technologies including .NET, Java, PHP and Node.js.

The new website should acknowledge this software-development heritage without making traditional software development the primary offering.

Possible positioning:

**"Decades of technology experience, focused on what's next."**

or

**"Built on decades of technology experience. Focused on the AI-powered future."**

Gilani Enterprises also has business interests and investments in:

* education in Pakistan
* vocational and technology education
* nonprofit educational initiatives
* retail operations in the United States

These activities are SECONDARY.

Do not put Education or Retail in the primary navigation and do not make them prominent homepage service areas.

They may be mentioned briefly on the About page to demonstrate broader real-world business and operating experience.

Do NOT publicly identify the retail businesses.

# 18. TECHNICAL SEO / PERFORMANCE

Because the final implementation will run on WordPress, keep the design technically lightweight.

Prioritize:

* Core Web Vitals
* minimal JavaScript
* optimized images
* WebP/AVIF where supported
* responsive images
* lazy-loading below-the-fold imagery
* explicit image dimensions
* minimal layout shifts
* optimized font loading
* limited font families and weights
* reusable CSS
* accessible navigation
* server-rendered/indexable page content
* no important text rendered only through JavaScript

Avoid heavy animation frameworks.

Animations should be subtle CSS-based transitions whenever practical.

Do not create giant autoplay background videos.
